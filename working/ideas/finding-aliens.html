Finding Aliens using Unnatural Shapes

Nature contains examples for a range of shapes:

  • The straight lines of a fissure in slate, a hanging liana or spider's web between two points
  • The spheres produced by gravity like a star or planet, or the smoothed pebbles from centuries of erosion under water
  • The cubes of pyrite crystals
  • The spirals of galaxies and seashells
  • The hexagons of beehives

However, finding true regular polygons (e.g. a regular hexagon or square face) at sizes much greater than that of an insect would seem rare if not missing.

This might be because regardless of whether the object was produced physically, chemically or biologically, the situation would need to be one of “perfect” uniformity without external disturbance.

For example imagine the structure is a crystal (i.e. chemical in nature). Crystals can grow in various shapes with exceptionally straight edges and defined angles, but even beyond a few centimetres most of their growth will be affected by other factors like their own weight, by changes in temperature and solute concentration, by impurities, by changing currents, and by competing crystal growth from other seeds. The chance of truly massive single crystals are low. Most of the “large” crystals we see in museums and caves are typically agglomerations of many smaller crystals that are stuck together and are not regular in the shape to the same degree.

If the structure is biological, there might be additional reasons we don’t see large regular shapes such as hexagons or cubes. Most regular structures without any supports or curves are not likely to be strong, and Nature seems to prefer curves (e.g. the approximately cuboid vascular tissues of some plants, the rounded points of hexagons in beehives, the tiny yet intricate coccolithophore shell structures… they all exhibit patterns that are rounded at any nodes, and most vertices are gently curved too. If we think of soft, squidgy cells are the basic repeating unit of any living structure, we can also hypothesise that creating close to perfect points and straight sides would be difficult. The reason the wall of a house can have a 90 degree bend is because the underlying bricks are made rectangular too.

Where am I going with this?

While Nature has (in my opinion) a small likelihood of ever producing truly large, regular structures… humans do it all the time deliberately:

  • The sides of skyscrapers
  • The precisely rectangular window panes of glass
  • The steel beams that form the core of buildings
  • The arrow-straight roads across flat terrain
  • The triangles of roofs
  • The tubes of under-sea cables

So, if other intelligent lifeforms have started building things, then finding such shapes at larger scales might be a good indicator of their presence?

If there were some spectral way of detecting large squares, cubes, triangles, hexagons etc etc… would it help us scan the galaxy for aliens?
